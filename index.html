The gap

Most people get their first real help in a crisis. It shouldn’t take a crisis.

Most of us learned geometry and grammar. No one taught us why our heart races before a hard conversation, and how to calm it down in that moment.

Behavioral health has self-help apps and crisis care, with very little in between. Wise Friend fills that gap with everyday skills people learn together.

1 in 5U.S. adults experience a mental health condition1
< 3 in 10managers have training to discuss mental health2
Our mission

Our mission at Wise Friend is to make behavioral health tools easy to understand and widely accessible, while empowering people to be their best selves and inspiring others to do the same.

What we do

Practical skills for everyday life.

We teach people how to handle pressure, support each other and know when to get more help. The tools draw on CBT, ACT and DBT, taught in plain language.

The tools

What people use.

The 101 Course

45 minutes on how stress works, quick ways to reset, and how to support someone else.

The Life Plan™

A written plan: where am I now, where do I want to go, and how do I get there?

Inspire Reflection Cards

Questions that start honest conversations, alone, with one person or in a group.

Wellbeing Census

A short survey so the program matches what people say they need.

Ways to work with us

Three levels. Five ways to start.

Start with Level 1 and go deeper from there. Mentorship and custom programs use the same tools.

Level 1 SystemMental Health Foundations and Life Plan Pathway

The same starting language and tools for everyone in your community.

  • The 101 Course
  • The Life Plan™ and Inspire Reflection Cards
  • Live sessions with your group

Level 2 SystemPeer Support Certification

A deeper course in mental and emotional fitness and supporting others.

  • Guided practice and assessment
  • Peer support certification
  • Earned by showing the skills

Level 3 SystemFacilitator Certification

Training to lead the program and train the next group.

  • Training to teach it
  • An observed first session
  • The rights to lead it in your setting
Alongside the levels · one to one, or built for you.

Mentorship

A one-to-one match with an experienced mentor, for adults and for young people.

  • Careful matching and screening
  • A retreat to start, then regular check-ins
  • A shared Life Plan™

Custom program

Designed with you for your team, school, league or community.

  • The levels and tools that fit your people
  • Timing that fits your calendar
  • Measurement you can report on

Interested in any of these options? We’ll help you find the right fit.
